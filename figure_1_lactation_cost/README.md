# Figure 1 - Mass-specific lactation cost

Reproduces the lactation-cost figure: the percentage of total daily energy loss
transferred to the calf (blue, left axis) and the mass-specific lactation cost
(orange, right axis), versus maternal length, at three resting proportions
(solid = empirical 0.4; dashed = 0.2 and 0.8).

**Run:** open `reviewer_package` in RStudio (or `setwd()` into this folder), then
`source("make_figure.R")`.

**Input:** `../data/processed/lactation_estimates.csv`
**Output:** `figure_1_lactation_cost.png` (written here)
**Packages:** dplyr, ggplot2
