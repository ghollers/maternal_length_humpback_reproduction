# Figure 3 - Maternal annual time budget

Reproduces the stacked-bar figure of days per year a mother spends on the
breeding ground (Hawaii), in migration, and on the feeding ground (Alaska), by
maternal length. Breeding-ground residency is the predicted calf departure age;
migration is fixed at 2 x 39 days (Mate et al. 1998); the remainder is feeding-
ground time.

**Run:** `source("make_figure.R")`.

**Input:** `../data/processed/departure_df.csv`
**Output:** `figure_3_annual_time_budget.png` (written here)
**Packages:** dplyr, tidyr, ggplot2
